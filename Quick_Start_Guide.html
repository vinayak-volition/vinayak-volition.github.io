Safety Audit Platform – Pilot v0.3

Quick Start Guide · Requirement library: Fire & Life Safety Audit Checklist NBC 2016 Ver3 (381 requirements: NBC 2016, IS standards, CEA Safety Regulations 2023) · Sample client programme: Decathlon EFSA

1. What changed from v0.2

Problemv0.3 answer
The checklist keeps growing as standards are added.Requirement library + audit programmes. All standards sit in one library. An audit uses a programme: the client's KPI set, chosen library sections, or both.
Clients such as Decathlon audit against their own KPIs.Client KPI programmes. Each client KPI becomes a question, a measurement, or a link to existing library requirements. Reports are grouped by the client's KPI groups.
Auditors page through hundreds of questions that do not apply.Scoping. About 15–30 yes/no building facts (height, basements, DG set, lifts, escalators…) set aside requirements that cannot apply, marked N/A with the reason. The auditor can override any of them.
Questions are ordered by code section, not by where the auditor is standing.Site walk-through. Questions grouped by site area (panel room, DG set, pump room, staircases, lifts…). Switch to KPI group or code section at any time.
Recording a compliant item takes several taps.Quick answers. Tap C on the question card; likelihood defaults to 1 (Rare, editable). PC, NC, NA and NV open the full question for the observation, justification and risk rating. "Mark the unanswered as Compliant" covers a whole area after checking.
Many client KPIs are readings, not yes/no checks.Measurement questions. Enter one or several readings (e.g. each earth pit); the status is set from the benchmark band: compliant, partially compliant, non-compliant. Readings appear in the Word and Excel reports.

Everything from v0.2 is kept: sign-in, separate Admin Console, user management, completed-audit register, admin dashboard, Word and Excel audit and closure reports, compliance score and separate risk score.

3. First-time setup

  1. Open Admin_Console.html. On a new device it shows First-time setup: create the System Administrator account.
  2. Users → Create user for each auditor, reviewer, Audit Administrator and management user (temporary password; they change it at first sign-in).
  3. Programmes: two are ready: Decathlon – Electrical & Fire Safety Audit (sample) with 168 questions, and the full Requirement library with 381.
  4. To try it at once: Data → Sample: Decathlon store creates a scoped and completed sample audit.

4. Running an audit

  1. Create (Audit Administrator, in the audit application): New audit → choose the programme, lead auditor and team.
  2. Scope: the app opens the scoping screen. Answer the building facts (Yes / No / ? for unknown). The counter shows how many questions will be set aside. Tap Apply scoping. You can come back and change answers at any time; requirements return to the audit if a fact changes.
  3. Walk-through: pick a site area. For each card:
    • C: recorded at once (likelihood 1 by default; open the card to change).
    • PC / NC: opens the question for observation, likelihood, recommendation, photos.
    • NA: opens for the justification. NV: opens for the reason.
    • Reading cards: open and enter readings; add rows for several points (e.g. Pit 1, Pit 2). The status follows the benchmark; you can set it manually, and "use result" restores the automatic status.
  4. Out-of-scope cards are hidden; tick "Show out-of-scope" in an area to see or override them.
  5. Submit, review (optional), reports and closure work as in v0.2.

5. Creating a programme for another client

  1. Admin Console → Programmes → Download client KPI file template.
  2. One row per client KPI. Type: Check (yes/no requirement), Measurement (numeric reading with compliant and partial bands), or Library item (fully covered by library requirement(s) in Library refs, e.g. P-16). Optional: severity, site area code, "applies when" scoping codes, reference, guidance.
  3. Programmes → New programme: name, client, choose the KPI file and, if wanted, whole library sections to add. Create, review, then Activate.
  4. Any question can be edited (wording, severity, weight, site area, scoping codes, measurement limits) until the programme is used by an audit. After that, use New version.

6. Scoring

Unchanged from v0.2. Compliance % = Σ(credit × weight) ÷ Σ(weight) over C, PC and NC; N/A (including out-of-scope) and NV are excluded. Overall rating from compliance alone: Satisfactory ≥ 90%, Needs improvement ≥ 75%, Unsatisfactory ≥ 50%, Unsafe below 50%. Risk = Severity × Likelihood for every applicable question, graded Low / Medium / High / Critical. Reports add compliance by site area, a measured-parameters table and the scoping answers.

7. Points to confirm in the Decathlon sample

  • Benchmarks marked "to be confirmed" come from the Decathlon reports or are auditor suggestions: 230 V ±6%, 415 V ±10%, phase imbalance ≤ 10%, power factor ≥ 0.95, THD-i ≤ 15%, THD-v ≤ 8%, N–E voltage < 1 V, panel room lighting ≥ 450 lux, 8–15 ACH, 23–25 °C. Earth pits use the Ver3 auditor benchmark (≤ 2 Ω compliant, ≤ 5 Ω partially compliant). Thermography, room temperature, neutral current and ARD battery readings are recorded without limits.
  • Severities of the Decathlon KPIs are my proposal (4 for earthing, shock, lift rescue and escalator stop items; 2 for energy and comfort items; 3 otherwise).
  • Site areas for the 381 library requirements were assigned automatically from section and wording, and scoping rules from the Applicability column (181 requirements). Requirements whose applicability depends on judgement (for example "where provided", occupancy lists) are left for the auditor.
  • Six Decathlon KPIs are now covered by Ver3 Section P and appear as those library items (PPE P-17, rubber mats P-16, danger notices P-15, colour coding P-12, temporary cables P-08, source identification P-19).

8. Pilot limitations

  • No server: each device has its own users and data; back up from Admin Console → Data. Data is not encrypted; protect devices with a screen lock.
  • Several auditors work on separate devices and merge audit packages (More → Export / Merge).
  • Camera capture, downloads and tablet/desktop layouts should be checked on your own devices before field use.
